DRAUN Music Player

Listening to your own offline FLAC collection shouldn't feel like piloting a spaceship. I built DRAUN because modern streaming apps feel like ad engines with play buttons. Inspired by Braun minimalism, vintage click-wheels, Cover Flow nostalgia, and zero-algorithm listening.
