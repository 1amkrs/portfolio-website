Pricing questions, answered.

Is GST included in the price?
The displayed price is exclusive of GST. We add 18% GST and provide a proper tax invoice on the 1st of each month.
What's the lock-in period?
None. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime before your next billing date.
Can I switch plans mid-month?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately - we pro-rate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
Do you offer corporate billing?
Yes. We can invoice your company directly with GST-compliant invoices. Contact us at hello@queworkspace.com to set it up.
Are founding-member prices locked forever?
Founding-member pricing is locked for the first 90 days or the first 20 members, whichever comes first. After that, standard pricing applies.
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI, bank transfer, debit/credit cards, and net banking. We use Razorpay for secure online payments.
