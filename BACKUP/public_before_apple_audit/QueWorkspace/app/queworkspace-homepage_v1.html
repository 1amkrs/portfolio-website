The obvious questions.

We keep the rules short and the small print honest.

Can I try before I sign up?
Yes. Book a tour and we'll walk you through the floor. If you like it, we'll throw in a free day pass to try a real working day before you decide.
Is there a long lock-in period?
No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel before your renewal date and you're free — no penalties, no guilt trips.
What's your cancellation and refund policy?
Cancel 7 days before renewal for a full stop. No partial refunds on the current month, but we'll always try to work something out.
Will I get a GST invoice?
Yes. Every payment generates a proper GST invoice in your company name, emailed to you automatically.
Can I register my company at this address?
Yes. Our GST + Incorporation plan includes NOC, rent agreement, and utility bill for company registration in Karnataka.
Can my team get a private room?
Teams of 6–10 get a private zone with the Team Plan, Larger. Smaller teams (3–5) get a dedicated cluster in the open floor.
Do you offer a virtual office address?
Yes. Our Address Only plan gives you a JP Nagar business address with mail handling starting at ₹999/mo.
How fast is the wifi?
500 Mbps symmetrical fibre. A backup line kicks in automatically if the primary ever drops.
Is there a quiet zone? Can I take calls?
The main floor is a quiet-ish zone — headphones culture. For calls, use the discussion room or the phone booth.
What about parking?
Basement parking available. Two-wheeler parking is free; four-wheeler spots on a first-come basis.
Are pets allowed on the floor?
Not yet, but we're working on a pet-friendly policy. For now, the plant in Quiet Room A is the closest thing to a mascot.
What are your hours?
Mon–Sat, 10am–8pm. Sundays by appointment. Members with a monthly plan get 24/7 keycard access.
Can guests visit me?
Yes, guests are welcome during staffed hours. Just let the front desk know.
What's the day pass price?
₹399 for a full day. Includes a hot desk, Wi-Fi, pantry access, and common areas.
How do I get there?
We're on Kothanur Main Road in JP Nagar 8th Phase. Google Maps will get you right to the door — look for SS Plaza 2, 2nd floor.
Still have questions? Write to us, we usually reply within a few hours.hello@queworkspace.com
