Projects / Kaira
Personal Project Work in Progress

Every time I sit down on the couch after a long day of designing, my TV feels less like a relaxing entertainment hub and more like an aggressive shopping mall. Between seven streaming apps, auto-playing trailers, and zero connection to my own media server or game library, just picking something to play or watch feels like work. Kaira is my personal project born out of that frustration: a calm, controller-first 10-foot media shell with 0 ads, designed to bring movies, TV shows, FLAC music, and future game library integration into one unified living room experience.

Craft & Focus Areas:

Controller-First UI 0 Ads & Zero Bloat 10-Foot Focus Engine Media & Game Hub tvOS Architecture Lossless Music Dock Personal R&D

The Friction (Challenge):

Modern Smart TVs are cluttered, sluggish with a gamepad, and treat your personal media and games like second-class citizens. Jumping between multiple launchers just to check your watchlist or boot up a game ruins the couch vibe.

My Role:

Designer & Developer. Engineering the gamepad/D-pad focus physics, ambient lighting bleed, zero-ad architecture, game launcher concepts, and the frontend shell prototype from scratch.

Platform & Status:

Living Room Interface & Design Explorations

Concept Vision & Reflections

Kaira started because I wanted a single, controller-friendly way to browse cinema, stream lossless music, and eventually launch my personal game collection from the sofa with zero ads. By focusing on tactile focus rings, dark glass layers, and absolute visual purity, Kaira aims to make turning on the TV feel like a pleasure again.

Controller-First UX Gamepad D-pad and analog stick navigation tuned to feel tactile, snappy, and effortless.
0 Ads No commercial clutter, sponsored banners, or forced popups. Pure media and games.
Unified Library Local server files, streaming platforms, and personal media coexisting in one dock.
Ambient Sync Gentle UI light bleeds that extend the movie mood into the physical room.
Future Game Hub Roadmap includes linking personal Steam, emulation, and PC game libraries directly to the shell.
Work In Progress Interactive WebGL prototype and controller mapping iterations in progress.

Designer Takeaways

  • Controller-first means zero dead zones: When navigating with a gamepad instead of a mouse, focus states are everything. Button grids require strict spatial alignment so the focus ring never gets trapped.
  • 0 ads makes living room tech peaceful: Technology in the living room shouldn't compete for your attention with commercial popups. A clean, ad-free interface makes the whole home feel better.
  • Focus states are the entire experience: Without a cursor or touch gestures, the active focus ring is how users feel your product. Smooth easing and clear outlines make all the difference.
