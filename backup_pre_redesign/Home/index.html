About Me

Karthik Satheesh

Product Designer

Product Designer with 4 years of experience crafting meaningful and user-first digital experiences. With a B.A. in Graphic Design & Animation, my passion for storytelling, visuals, and problem-solving naturally led me into product design.

I began my journey at Katzion, where I spent 2.5 years honing my skills in user research, interaction design, and product thinking, turning complex problems into simple, delightful solutions. I built impactful digital experiences at Kaizen Que, expanding my craft in creating thoughtful and engaging products.

Beyond design, you’ll find me strumming the guitar, sketching random ideas, or heading off on spontaneous travels. For me, great design sits perfectly at the intersection of empathy, function, and creativity.
